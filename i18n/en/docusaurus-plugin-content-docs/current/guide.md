# User Guide

The StoaZen app has five tabs: Draw, Meditation, Saved, Progress and Settings.

## Draw

1. Answer "What is troubling you right now?" by picking one of the eight themes, or "Whatever Comes".
2. Tap "Draw a card".
3. Tap the card to flip it and read the plain-language reading. You can save it, share it, or draw another.

There's no daily limit on draws. The app gives you cards you haven't seen first. Only after you've seen every card in a theme will cards you've seen come back, marked "Again". It also avoids the last few cards you drew.

## Meditation

Three silent practices, each for 1, 5, 10 or 20 minutes:

- **Breath**: breathe in and out with the rise and fall of the ink circle
- **Count**: count once on each out-breath, from one to ten, then start again
- **Just Sitting**: do nothing at all, just sit

Meditation has no voice guidance and no background music. There's one gentle vibration at the start and one at the end, and the screen stays on throughout. Card pages also have a "Sit for a minute" shortcut.

Meditation is logged in your calendar and total time, but it **earns no XP and doesn't count toward your streak**.

## Saved

Tap save on a card and it stays in the Saved tab. You can filter by theme or school, and search quotes and authors by keyword.

## Progress

- **Growth**: your level, XP progress, draw streak, Collection (how many cards you've seen), badges, History, and total meditation time
- **Calendar**: the cards you drew and the number of meditations each day. Tap a marked date to see that day's cards. It also shows which theme you drew for most this month.

## XP and levels

| Action | XP |
| --- | --- |
| First draw of the day | 20 |
| Drawing a new card | 10 |
| Flipping a card to read it | 5 |
| Saving a card | 5 |
| Drawing a card you've seen | 2 |

You can earn up to 60 XP a day, but there's no limit on drawing itself. When your streak reaches 3, 7, 30 and 100 days, you get a milestone bonus that doesn't count against the daily cap.

Levels only unlock looks. They never lock any content. Card styles unlocked by level:

| Level | Card style |
| --- | --- |
| Lv1 | Rice Paper |
| Lv2 | Linen |
| Lv3 | Ink Wash |
| Lv5 | Bamboo Slip |
| Lv8 | Marble |
| Lv11 | Silk |
| Lv16 | Gold Leaf |
| Lv20 | Sutra Paper |

## Daily reminder

Turn on the daily reminder in Settings and pick a time and a theme. Each day you'll get a notification with a quote from that theme. Tap it to see the full card. If notifications for StoaZen are turned off in your system settings, the Settings page gives you a shortcut there.

## Language

The interface and all the quotes are available in Traditional Chinese and English. You can switch in Settings.
