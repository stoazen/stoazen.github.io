# Explore StoaZen

This is where you'll find StoaZen's content and how-to guides. Want to know where the quotes come from, which theme fits which situation, or how a feature in the app works? Start here.

## Content

- [Three schools](./schools.md): Zen, Stoic philosophy and psychology, how each one answers what's troubling you, and the texts and authors they draw on
- [Eight themes](./themes.md): from Anxiety to Letting Go, the situations each theme is for, with sample quotes

## Using the app

- [User guide](./guide.md): Draw, Meditation, Saved, Progress, levels and the daily reminder
- [FAQ](./faq.md): cost, accounts, offline use and where your data is kept

## A few principles behind the content

- Every card names its author and source, and comes with a plain-language reading, in both English and Chinese.
- The content comes from public-domain texts and short, quotable lines.
- We keep adding to and refining the quotes. When you're online, the app updates in the background without getting in your way.
