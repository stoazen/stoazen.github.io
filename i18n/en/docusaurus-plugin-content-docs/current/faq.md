# FAQ

## Does StoaZen cost anything?

The app is free to download and use. You never have to pay to unlock any of the quotes.

## When can I download it?

The iOS version is out now on the App Store. The Android version is coming soon to Google Play, and a browser extension is in the works. We'll announce new releases on the [home page](/#download).

## Do I need to sign up for an account?

No. There's no account system for now. Just open the app and start.

## Can I use it offline?

Yes. The first time you open the app, it needs an internet connection to download the quotes. After that, it works offline. When you're online, the app checks for content updates in the background.

## Where is my data stored?

Your saved cards, draw history, level and settings are stored only on your phone. They're never uploaded. That also means there's no syncing across devices for now. If you delete the app or use "Reset data" in Settings, your history can't be recovered.

## Why doesn't meditation earn XP?

Streaks and rewards create pressure not to break the chain. For a tool meant to help you calm down, that backfires. So meditation is simply logged, never scored.

## Are the quote sources reliable?

Every card names its author and source. The content comes from public-domain texts and short, quotable lines. If you spot a mistake, please let us know by [email](mailto:stoazen.com@gmail.com).
