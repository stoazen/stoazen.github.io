# About StoaZen

StoaZen gathers wise words from Zen, Stoic philosophy and psychology. It's made for you, if you're feeling troubled, anxious or stuck in life.

The name comes from **Stoa** and **Zen**. Two thousand years and half a world apart, they ask the same question: when you can't control what happens around you, how do you find your footing?

## What we do

At its heart, StoaZen is a pocket of wisdom you can reach into. When something is weighing on you, pick the trouble closest to how you feel right now and draw a card, and let a single line help you see things from a new angle. When you want to settle down, sit for a minute with your breath and bring your attention back to the present.

There are 180 quotes so far, from three schools:

- **Zen**: classic Zen texts such as *The Platform Sutra*, *Faith in Mind*, *The Record of Linji* and *The Gateless Gate*
- **Stoic philosophy**: Epictetus, Seneca, Marcus Aurelius
- **Psychology**: William James, Viktor Frankl, Sigmund Freud, Carl Rogers, Alfred Adler, Carl Jung and others

The content comes from public-domain texts and short, quotable lines. Every card names its author and source, and comes with a plain-language reading, in both English and Chinese.

## What we stand by

- **No content behind a wall.** Levels only unlock looks. They never hold back a single card.
- **Meditation is logged, not scored.** Getting quiet shouldn't come with the pressure of keeping a streak alive.
- **Your data stays on your phone.** No account needed. Your saved cards and history live only on your device.
