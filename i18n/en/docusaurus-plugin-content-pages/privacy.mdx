# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 11, 2026

This policy explains how the StoaZen mobile app and the website www.stoazen.com (together, "StoaZen", "we" or "us") handle your data. In short: the app doesn't need an account, your usage history stays only on your device, and we don't collect or upload it.

## 1. The app does not collect personal data

The StoaZen app has no account system. It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other information that could identify you. The app contains no built-in advertising, behavioral analytics or third-party tracking tools.

## 2. Data stored on your device

The following data, created as you use the app, is stored only in a local database on your device. It is not sent to us or to any third party:

- Saved cards
- Draw history, XP, level, streak and badges
- Meditation history (number of sessions and time)
- Settings: language, card style, and the time and theme of your daily reminder

Because this data is not uploaded, it cannot currently be synced or backed up across devices.

## 3. Network connections

The app connects to the internet only in the following cases:

- **Downloading quote content**: the first time you open the app, and afterward when it checks in the background for content updates, the app downloads quotes, themes and appearance data from our content server (api.stoazen.com). These requests do not include your usage history or any personal data.
- As with any website, the server and its hosting provider may automatically log technical details of the connection (such as IP address and request time). This is used only to keep the service running and to troubleshoot problems.

Apart from this, the app can be used completely offline.

## 4. Device permissions

- **Notifications**: requested only if you turn on the "Daily reminder". Reminders are scheduled and delivered locally by your device and do not pass through our servers. On Android, the app reschedules reminders after the device restarts.
- **Keeping the screen on**: during a meditation session, the app temporarily keeps the screen on, and returns to normal when the session ends.

The app does not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts or photo library.

## 5. Sharing cards

When you choose to share a card, the app creates an image of the card on your device and passes it, through your system's share menu, to the app you choose. We do not receive what you share or who you share it with.

## 6. Website

The website uses Google Tag Manager and related Google analytics services to understand how many people visit and how pages are used. These services may use cookies and collect anonymized usage information, such as browser type and pages visited. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

The newsletter signup form on the home page is provided by Substack. The email address you enter in the form is sent directly to Substack and handled under [Substack's privacy policy](https://substack.com/privacy). We use it only to send the newsletter, and you can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email.

The website's site search runs in your browser. Your search terms are not sent to our servers.

## 7. Browser extension

The StoaZen browser extension has not been released yet. Before it launches, we will add to this policy how the extension handles data.

## 8. Deleting your data

- In the app, go to "Settings → Reset data" to clear all of your XP, level, streak, saved cards and draw history. This cannot be undone.
- When you delete the app, all StoaZen data on your device is removed with it.

## 9. Children's privacy

StoaZen does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

## 10. Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will post the changes on this page and update the effective date. Significant changes (for example, adding accounts or syncing in the future) will be explained separately before they take effect.

## 11. Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us by [email](mailto:stoazen.com@gmail.com).
